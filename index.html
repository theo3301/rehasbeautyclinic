ABOUT US

Welcome to Reha’s, where beauty meets passion. Founded by Reha, whose love for beauty started in the picturesque valleys of Srinagar, our salon is a culmination of her lifelong dream. With extensive training and certifications from renowned institutions like the Harshada Takke Skin Care Certification Board and the Jaywant Thakre Makeup Academy, Reha brings unmatched expertise to every service.
Our offerings include exceptional skin care, stunning nail art, and professional hair styling, honed under the guidance of Bollywood hairstylist Kamal Palan. Reha’s work has graced fashion shows, the Zee Cine Awards, Bigg Boss, and numerous weddings and music albums.
At Reha’s, we are dedicated to making you look and feel your best. Join us and experience the artistry and care that define our brand.
