Company history

Welcome to Reha’s, where a dream has blossomed into reality. From a young age, Reha was captivated by the art of beauty, seeing it as a form of engineering. Growing up amidst the breathtaking landscapes of Srinagar, she absorbed the natural beauty around her, which became an integral part of her being.
Reha's dedication to her skincare routine was meticulous, always ensuring she looked her best. This inner passion evolved into a full-fledged career in the beauty industry, making her an unstoppable force today.

Reha's journey is marked by numerous accomplishments and certifications:

Certified Professional in Skin Care:
Reha earned her credentials from the prestigious Harshada Takke Skin Care Certification Board.

Nail Art Expertise:
Her passion and talent for nail art have established her as an excellent nail artist.

Hair Styling Proficiency:
Trained by the renowned Bollywood hairstylist Kamal Palan, Reha's skills in hair styling are unmatched.

Makeup Mastery:
She realized her dream of being trained by the Jaywant Thakre Makeup Academy, one of India's premier makeover academies

Reha’s expertise extends to high-profile events and projects, including:


  • Fashion shows with choreographer Shakir Sheikh.
  • The Zee Cine Awards.
  • Bigg Boss with Amisha Patel.
  • Numerous music albums and weddings.

At Reha’s, we are committed to bringing the same level of passion and excellence to each client, ensuring they experience the beauty and care that Reha herself embodies.
